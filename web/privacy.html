Privacy Policy

Cruxe · Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what Cruxe collects, why, and who processes it. It mirrors the policy shown inside the app; if the two ever disagree, please tell us and we will correct it.

1. What we collect

  • Account data. Every install gets an anonymous identifier so your progress can be saved. If you choose to link a Google or Apple account, we also store the name and email that provider gives us. Linking is optional — the game is fully playable without it.
  • Gameplay data. Puzzle completions, scores, solve times, streaks and coin balance. These make the leaderboard and your statistics work.
  • Purchase records. If you buy a coin pack, we record that it happened so the coins can be granted and a refund honoured. We never see your card details — Google Play handles payment.
  • Diagnostics. Crash reports and basic device information (model, OS version), plus a record of which screens and actions preceded a crash. Your device name is deliberately removed before anything is sent.

2. What we never collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, calendar, browsing history, or advertising identifier. Cruxe contains no advertising and no advertising SDK.

3. Who processes your data

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • RevenueCat — validating in-app purchases. Receives your anonymous account identifier and purchase history.
  • Sentry — crash reporting and diagnostics. Receives device model, OS version and your anonymous identifier.
  • Google or Apple Sign-In — only if you choose to link an account.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

4. Your display name is public

The name you choose appears on the global leaderboard alongside your score and streak. Nothing else about you is shown. You can change it at any time in your profile.

5. Deleting your data

Open Cruxe, go to Profile → Delete account. This is immediate and permanent: your profile, progress, streak, coin balance and leaderboard entry are erased. Full instructions, including what to do if you no longer have the app installed, are on the account deletion page.

Purchase records may be retained in anonymised form where tax and accounting law requires it. They cannot be linked back to you.

6. Children

Cruxe is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.
