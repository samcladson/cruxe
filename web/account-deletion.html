Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Cruxe
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll to Delete account
  4. Confirm when prompted

Deletion is immediate and permanent. No waiting period, no reactivation.

If you no longer have the app installed

Email samcladson08@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject Cruxe account deletion. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

Every Cruxe account is created with Google, Apple, or an email code, so we can find yours from the address you write from. If you signed in with Apple and chose Hide My Email, say so in your message and include your Cruxe display name so we can locate the account.

Before you delete

Any coins remaining in your balance, including purchased coins, are forfeited and cannot be refunded or transferred. Deleting your account does not cancel or refund past purchases. This cannot be undone.
