Delete Your Cruxe Account

Cruxe · developed by Sam Cladson

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Cruxe
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll to Delete account
  4. Confirm when prompted

Deletion is immediate and permanent. No waiting period, no reactivation.

If you no longer have the app installed

Email samcladson08@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject Cruxe account deletion. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

If you signed in anonymously and never linked Google or Apple, there is no identifier that ties the account to you, so we cannot locate it from an email. In that case reinstall the app and delete from within it, or simply leave it — anonymous accounts hold no personal data.

What is deleted

Data What happens
Account and sign-in identity Deleted immediately
Display name and profile Deleted immediately
Puzzle history, scores, streaks Deleted immediately
Coin balance and transaction history Deleted immediately
Leaderboard entry Removed immediately
Purchase receipts Retained in anonymised form where tax and accounting law requires it. These cannot be linked back to you.

Before you delete

Any coins remaining in your balance, including purchased coins, are forfeited and cannot be refunded or transferred. Deleting your account does not cancel or refund past purchases. This cannot be undone.
