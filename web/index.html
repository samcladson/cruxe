How it plays

Not just across and down.

Some Cruxe answers read backwards. Some read upwards. An arrow on every clue tells you which way it runs, so the grid is fair. You just have to stop assuming.

→ ACROSS↓ DOWN← BACK↑ UP

What's inside

Built for people who like the puzzle.

Clean grids, clues with a bit of bite, and nothing between you and the next answer.

A new set, every day

A Daily Challenge plus a full collection across General, History, Technology, Entertainment and Sports.

Four tiers, four sizes

Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert, from a gentle 6×6 to a properly demanding 12×12.

Hints you earn

Reveal a letter, uncover a word, or check the grid for mistakes. Hints cost coins, and you earn coins by solving. Nobody has to pay to finish a puzzle.

Streaks that forgive, once

Solve daily and your streak and daily bonus grow. Miss a day and you can restore it, free once a month.

Graded S to D

Every solve is scored on difficulty, speed and how few hints you used. Climb the global leaderboard.

A takeaway with every puzzle

Finish a grid and get a short paragraph on its theme, with the answers you just found worked into it.

Plays offline

Solve on a train or a plane. Progress and rewards sync when you reconnect.

No ads. Ever.

Not between puzzles, not for rewards, not anywhere. Coin packs are entirely optional.
